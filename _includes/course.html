Connect your federation to Equipe

You build the web services and Equipe calls them. We import your shows and entries, look up riders, horses, officials and clubs during the show, and send you the results when it's over.

Six steps to a full integration

  1. 1 Sign in users Your users log in to Equipe from your system. We create the organizer and the user.
  2. 2 Import shows and entries We read your list of shows (2a), then the entries of the show the user picks (2b).
  3. 3 Map judgements Match your judgement ids with ours in the federation settings.
  4. 4 Search riders, horses, officials and clubs For the changes that come in during the show.
  5. 5 Receive results We send them to you when the show is over.
  6. 6 Translate the apps app.equipe.com and online.equipe.com in your language.
